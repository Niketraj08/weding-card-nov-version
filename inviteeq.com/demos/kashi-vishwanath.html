Through the lanes of Kashi

Our story

  1. Chapter 1

    Where it began

    A rain-soaked evening at Assi Ghat, one umbrella between two strangers, a kulhad of chai each, and an argument about the best kachori in the lanes that neither of us has won to this day.

  2. Chapter 2

    The long way round

    Two cities, a hundred late-night calls and one very patient family group chat later, we kept finding our way back to the river.

  3. Chapter 3

    The question

    On a winter morning out on the Ganga, with temple bells carrying across the water, Vedant asked. Gauri said yes before he could finish.

  4. Chapter 4

    Forever begins

    Now we come home to Kashi to begin our life together, with all of you beside us.

…and every lane led us here.
